# Fortumars Job Portal - Comprehensive User Manual

Welcome to the Fortumars Job Portal! This platform is designed specifically for Tirupur's industrial ecosystem, seamlessly connecting skilled and unskilled workers with factory owners and administrative hubs.

## Overview & Benefits
The platform's mobile-first design philosophy ensures that Job Seekers—even those with limited technical proficiency—can easily browse and apply for jobs.
For Employers and Admins, the dashboard provides a powerful, real-time command center to manage the entire lifecycle of job postings, verify credentials, and onboard new talent.

### Pros and Benefits
- **Mobile-First Experience**: 95% of Job Seekers access the internet via mobile devices. Our PWA-ready design ensures an app-like experience without the friction of downloads.
- **Bilingual Interface**: Support for both English and Tamil ensures absolute clarity for the local workforce in Tirupur.
- **Strict Job Lifecycle**: Jobs are automatically managed. Expired or closed jobs are strictly removed from public visibility to ensure a clean, relevant feed for candidates.
- **Secure Verification**: An admin-approved verification system provides blue tick badges for trusted employers, building confidence in the platform.

---

## Job Seekers Workflow

1. **Onboarding**: Start by providing your basic details and a photo. You can specify whether you belong to the Technical or Non-Technical workforce.
2. **Browsing Jobs**: Use the Home or Search page to explore active job postings. Jobs that are closed or whose interview dates have passed are hidden automatically to save your time.
3. **Applying**: Click on a job to view comprehensive details (including shift timings, overtime pay, ESI, food, and accommodation). Click **Apply Now**. If an interview date has passed, the button will securely lock, indicating "🔒 Interview Passed".
4. **Tracking**: The "My Applications" tab keeps track of your history.

---

## Employers Workflow

1. **Registration & Approval**: Upon registering, your account must be verified by a Global Admin before you can post jobs.
2. **Posting a Job**: Click "Post Job" from the Employer Dashboard. Fill in accurate details including salary, shift timings, and benefits (like Free Transport or Hostel).
3. **Managing Jobs**: In the "Manage Jobs" tab, you can view your active listings. 
4. **Archiving Jobs**: If you have filled a position or wish to halt recruitment, you can archive a job. Archived jobs are immediately hidden from the public feed but remain in your dashboard history for future auditing.

---

## Global Admin Workflow

1. **Dashboard Overview**: The Admin Dashboard is the central command center.
2. **Profile Tab**: Go to **My Profile** to view your Role and Email. You can safely update your Full Name and Date of Birth here.
3. **Admin Management Tab**: Under **Admin Mgmt**, you can see all active Super Admins. To add a new Admin, use the form provided. *Note: Admin provisioning securely uses a secondary Firebase tunnel, meaning you won't be logged out of your current session while creating a new account.*
4. **Job Registry**: Approve new employer job postings before they go live. Review suspended or reported assets to maintain the ecosystem's integrity.
5. **Data Export**: Use the "Job Export" tab to download daily CSV audits for record-keeping.
